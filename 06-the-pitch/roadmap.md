## Board Pitch

**Thesis (1 sentence):**

AIM Intelligence can turn AIM's existing customer data and throughput-accounting expertise into personalized decision support that becomes more valuable as it develops a deeper understanding of each customer's business.

**The case:**

1. **Why now:**
   Customers have access to increasingly powerful general-purpose AI, but those tools do not automatically understand their business, cash-flow patterns, operational constraints, or AIM data. AIM has an opportunity to embed AI directly into the workflow where that context already exists.

2. **What's defensible:**
   The advantage is not the underlying AI model or the ability to explain throughput accounting. The advantage is the customer-specific context AIM can build over time — including historical cash-flow patterns, operational constraints, prior recommendations, assumptions, decisions, and eventually outcomes. A generic AI can understand the theory; AIM Intelligence can understand how that theory applies to a specific customer's business.

3. **The economics:**
   AIM can build on data and workflows already present in the product rather than creating a standalone AI experience. If customers find the recommendations valuable, AIM Intelligence can support premium pricing, stronger retention, and deeper product usage while managing AI costs through model routing and controlled use of higher-cost models.

**The risks:**

1. **Trust / failure modes:**
   Incorrect or overconfident recommendations could reduce customer trust or lead to poor decisions. AIM Intelligence should remain decision support rather than autonomous decision-making, show assumptions and uncertainty, provide multiple options when appropriate, and allow users to make the final decision.

2. **Scale / governance:**
   As usage grows, AIM will need reliable evaluation, monitoring, configurable confidence thresholds, data governance, auditability, and clear escalation processes to ensure quality does not degrade as models, customers, and use cases change.

3. **Competitive:**
   General-purpose AI providers and accounting software competitors can add similar AI capabilities. AIM cannot depend on access to an LLM as its differentiation. Its advantage must come from workflow integration, throughput-accounting expertise, customer-specific context, and a learning loop that improves recommendations as the customer continues using the product.

**The ask:**

Fund a staged investment in AIM Intelligence: validate customer value with explainable throughput-accounting insights in Horizon 1, invest in contextual recommendation capabilities in Horizon 2, and only pursue adaptive decision support in Horizon 3 if customer adoption, trust, economics, and evidence of a compounding data advantage justify the investment.
