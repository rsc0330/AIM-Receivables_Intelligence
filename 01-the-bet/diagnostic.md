# Three-Axis Vulnerability Diagnostic

## Product

**Product:** AIM Intelligence
**Your Role:** Product Manager / AI Product Strategy Lead

---

## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 4/5

*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:**
AIM Intelligence is designed to sit inside existing financial and operational workflows rather than operate as a standalone AI assistant. Its value comes from understanding the customer's business data, constraints, throughput-accounting logic, historical decisions, and operating context. As integrations and decision history deepen, switching becomes more difficult because a competitor would need to recreate not only the functionality but also the accumulated business context. The moat is not yet a 5 because customers could still switch if AIM fails to become deeply embedded in recurring decision workflows.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Intuit / QuickBooks — particularly if it adds similar AI-driven business decision support directly into the accounting workflow.

---

### Data Advantage — 3/5

*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:**
AIM has access to customer-specific operational and financial context that a general-purpose model does not inherently possess. The stronger long-term opportunity is to capture which recommendations were presented, which decisions customers made, the assumptions behind those decisions, and the resulting business outcomes. That could create a proprietary decision-and-outcome dataset that improves recommendations over time. Today, however, that advantage is still emerging because the feedback loop depends on consistently capturing outcomes and using them to improve the product.
